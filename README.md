## FAQs

<details>
<summary><strong>Where do these internships come from?</strong></summary>

A GitHub Action runs [`scraper/`](./scraper) every 2 hours. It calls the public job-board APIs (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) of every company in [`companies.json`](./companies.json), keeps the roles that are internships / co-ops / placements, sorts them into categories, and commits the changes. Community submissions via issues are merged too.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How are roles categorized?</strong></summary>

From the job title: 🔬 Research & Lab Science · 🧬 Computational Biology, Data & AI · 🏭 Engineering, Manufacturing & Quality · 🩺 Clinical, Regulatory & Medical Affairs · 💼 Business, Commercial & Operations. If one looks wrong, open an **Edit Internship** issue.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>My company / a company I want isn't tracked.</strong></summary>

Open an **[Add a Company](../../issues/new?template=add_company.yaml)** issue with its careers page. Once approved, the bot finds its job board and starts polling it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>A listing is closed / broken / wrong.</strong></summary>

Open an **[Edit Internship](../../issues/new?template=edit_internship.yaml)** issue. The bot also closes roles automatically once they disappear from the company's board.
</details>
